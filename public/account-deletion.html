Delete Your Muv'it Account

Last updated: 11 October 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Open Muv'it and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings, then Account Information.
  3. Select Delete Account and follow the request instructions.
  4. If you cannot sign in, email semogrouptech@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Include your Muv'it username and the phrase “account deletion request.”

What is deleted

When an account deletion request is verified and completed, Muv'it deletes or de-identifies the account profile, profile details, uploaded Muv'z and other content that remains under Muv'it's control, comments, social connections, notification preferences, and account-linked data that is no longer needed to operate the service.

What may be retained

Limited information may be retained when required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, enforcement of our Terms, or legitimate record-keeping. Reposted or quoted public content may remain visible where it has been incorporated into another user's content, subject to applicable law. Retained records are kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes.

Questions

For help with a deletion request or privacy question, contact semogrouptech@gmail.com.
